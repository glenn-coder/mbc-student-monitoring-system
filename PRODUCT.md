# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Laravel, Tailwind CSS, Vite

## Users

- **Administrators**: Manage the system, oversee records, users, and audit logs.
- **Instructors**: Enter and monitor student grades, attendance, and schedules.
- **Students**: View their progress, schedules, and academic standing.

## Product Purpose

MBC Student Monitoring System exists to track and manage the complete academic lifecycle of students. Success means accurate, real-time visibility into student performance and attendance.

## Positioning

It focuses specifically on monitoring student progress, attendance, and comprehensive audit trails, rather than being just a generic school management system.

## Operating Context

Used by school staff and students on desktop and mobile browsers to manage daily academic operations, grading, and reporting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Must integrate with existing Laravel backend features and database structures.

## Brand Commitments

- Preserve the existing MBC branding and navy-blue color identity.
- Maintain existing typography and clean modern admin-dashboard design.
- Keep the current sidebar and header layout.
- Use established semantic action colors.
- Maintain responsive layout and existing functionality.
- Do not change the established visual identity unless necessary for consistency.
